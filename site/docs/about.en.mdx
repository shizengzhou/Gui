<DocHead title="About Gui" />

<Prose>

Gui is a fast, feature-rich terminal emulator for Windows: platform-native UI, GPU-accelerated rendering, with a parsing core from Ghostty. It is Ghostty on Windows, and then some.

Why build Gui?

> As a project, we've decided to drop any roadmap for Windows support in the Ghostty GUI.

Because the Ghostty team doesn't plan to do it. Spending my tokens means you don't have to spend yours. Judging by Ghostty's technical choices on the other two platforms, I think they would pick WinUI if they did it officially, so I picked WinUI.

</Prose>
